# Income Statement (Chapter 3, Charter Communications)

- Three names for the same statement: income statement, statement of operations, statement of profit and loss
- Structure: Revenue → Operating Costs → EBIT → Interest Expense → EBT → Tax → Net Income
- Key terms:
  - EBIT = operating income = income from operations (measures operating efficiency)
  - EBT = earnings before income tax (less commonly used)
  - Net income = bottom line profit
- EBIT preferred over net income for measuring operating efficiency
  - Interest and tax excluded: vary by capital structure and geography, not operations
- 2025 figures (Charter Communications):
  - Revenue: $54,774M
  - Operating income (EBIT): $12,828M
  - Net income: $9,441M
- EPS (earnings per share) = net income ÷ basic shares outstanding
  - Basic shares: 135,155,309
  - Diluted shares: 137,743,677 (includes unexercised stock options)
  - Always use basic shares for EPS unless investor specifies diluted
  - Unit: dollars per share

# Statement of Shareholders’ Equity and Cash Flow

- Shareholders’ equity statement stacks 3 years by rows (not columns like balance sheet)
- 2025 highlights (Charter Communications):

  - Largest positive driver: consolidated net income
  - Largest negative driver: distribution to parent company
- Cash flow statement: 3 sections
  - Operating activities: cash from core business
  - Investing activities: typically negative (capital expenditure on PP\&E)
  - Financing activities: debt repayments, distributions to parent

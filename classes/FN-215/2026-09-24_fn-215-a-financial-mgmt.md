# Stock Market Data: Reading a Quote

- Bid vs. ask price
  - Bid: highest price buyers will pay (e.g., $5.51/share for JetBlue)
  - Ask: lowest price sellers will accept (e.g., $5.52/share)
  - Bid-ask spread: better liquidity measure than volume alone; reflects information asymmetry
- Trading volume as a liquidity proxy
  - High volume = liquid stock, easy to find buyers/sellers
  - JetBlue example: daily volume well above 3-month average of 18M shares, signaling investor optimism
- Key metrics on a stock quote
  - 52-week range, market cap, beta, EPS, P/E ratio, dividend info, 1-year price target
  - Market cap = price × shares outstanding; rough measure of firm value
- Home Depot live example (NYSE, \~$289/share)
  - Trading volume at 1:10 PM: \~2.76M shares
  - Market cap: \~$292.7B
  - Today’s high: $299.10

# Beta and Profitability Metrics

- Beta measures sensitivity of a stock’s return to market return
  - Beta &gt; 1: more volatile than market (JetBlue at 1.8)
  - Beta \&lt; 1: less volatile than market (Home Depot at 0.95)
  - Benchmark is always 1
- EPS (earnings per share): profit attributable per share
  - JetBlue EPS: -$0.55 (losing money); higher EPS = more profitable
- P/E ratio = price ÷ EPS
  - Reflects investor confidence; higher P/E = willing to pay more than book value suggests
  - Risk: can indicate overconfidence or overvaluation
- JetBlue 1-year analyst price target: $4.62, below current price, signaling analyst pessimism

# Balance Sheet Structure

- Fundamental equation: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders’ Equity
- Balance sheet is cumulative from firm inception to present
- Five sections to identify:
  1. Current assets (liquid, convertible within 1 year: cash, inventory, accounts receivable)
  2. Non-current assets (illiquid, &gt;1 year to convert: property, plant, equipment)
  3. Current liabilities (obligations due within 1 year)
  4. Non-current liabilities (long-term obligations, e.g., bonds payable over 5-10 years)
  5. Shareholders’ equity (assets minus liabilities; what remains for shareholders)
- Reading the layout
  - Single underline = subtotal of a subcategory
  - Double underline = total of a major category
  - Indentation shows hierarchy level

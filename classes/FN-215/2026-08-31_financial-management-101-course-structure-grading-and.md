## Summary

# Course Structure: Three Content Areas

- Financial markets and institutions (Ch. 1–4)
  - Major players, financial markets, institutions, and their market functions
  - Financial statements: balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, shareholders' equity
- Investments (Ch. 5–9)
  - Bonds, stocks, risk and return, time value of money, compounding
- Corporate finance (Ch. 10–11)
  - Capital budgeting and cost of capital
  - Perspective shift: student as CFO/financial manager making investment decisions
- Core goal: understand the *meaning* behind numbers and calculations, not just memorize formulas

# Grading Breakdown

- Exam 1 (Ch. 1–4, financial accounting): 25%
- Exam 2 (comprehensive, all chapters): 30%
- Assignments (8 total): 35%
- News discussion: 5%
- Attendance: 5%

# Exams, Assignments, and News Discussion

- Exams
  - Online via Blackboard with Lockdown Browser, taken in-person in classroom
  - Format: 15–20 multiple choice + 10–15 short answer/calculation (~40 questions total)
  - Closed book; formula sheet provided; calculator and scrap paper allowed
  - Mock test released one week before each exam, unlimited attempts, ungraded
- Assignments
  - 8 total, released Thursdays, due following Thursday at 11:59 PM
  - Unlimited attempts until 100%
  - Late within 24 hours: 10% deduction; after 24 hours: not graded
  - Extensions require 48-hour advance notice
- News discussion (5%)
  - Read WSJ and NYT via free SHU complimentary accounts (register with SHU email)
  - Rotating captains (3/week, alphabetical by last name) post: article link, one-sentence summary, a notable quote or number, and an open-ended discussion question
  - First-week captains: Nicholas, Caitlin, Emily; post due this Thursday
  - All others comment on one post; follower deadline for Discussion 1 is September 10th
  - Non-captains comment on one post per week; early posts allowed

# Bonus Points and Logistics

- Attendance bonus
  - Perfect attendance: +2 points to final grade
  - One absence: +1 point; two or more: no bonus, no penalty
  - 2 free unexcused absences; athletes/medical absences require coach email or doctor's note
- Participation bonus
  - Answer or ask a question in class, then fill out the form after class: +1 point per class
  - Points before Exam 1 added to Exam 1 score; points in second half added to Exam 2 score
- Pop-up questions
  - Occasional in-class paper questions (5–10 min) after key chapters (Ch. 4, 5, 9)
  - Correct answer: +1 point per question; wrong answer: no penalty
- Event bonus: finance-relevant events (career fairs, guest speakers) announced as they arise
- Historical grade context: average was 72 without bonus points, 86 with; distribution skews polarizing
- Textbook: not required ($146); professor's slides are sufficient
- Excel required: desktop software (not online version); available free via student account or Pioneer Tech (West Building, 1st floor); Mac users must use Excel, not Numbers

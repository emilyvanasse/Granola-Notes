## Summary

# Asset Management Ratios

- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO): 46 days vs. 36-day industry benchmark
  - Takes 10 extra days to collect receivables: weak credit policy
  - High DSO = slow cash collection; one of few ratios where lower is better
- Fixed assets turnover: 3x (slightly above average)
  - Every $1 in PP\&E generates $3 in sales revenue
  - Fixed assets = tangible long-term assets (PP\&E, capex); excludes intangibles like patents/goodwill
- Total assets turnover: 1.5x (below industry average)
  - Root causes: excess inventory and slow receivables collection
  - Approach: calculate total assets turnover first for overall efficiency, then drill into inventory, receivables, and fixed assets

# Debt Management (Solvency) Ratios

- Debt-to-total capital ratio: 47.8% vs. \~33% industry average
  - Nearly half of capital sourced from debt holders: costly and riskier
  - Debt = obligations with interest (notes payable, long-term bonds); excludes non-interest liabilities
  - Higher ratio = more leveraged = worse (in this course’s simplified framework)
- Times Interest Earned (TIE): 3.3x vs. 6x industry average
  - For every $1 of interest, only $3.30 of operating income (EBIT) generated
  - Industry peers generate $6 per $1 of interest: significantly stronger debt coverage
  - Higher TIE = stronger position to service debt (opposite interpretation from debt-to-capital)
- Key distinction: higher debt-to-capital is worse; higher TIE is better

# Profitability Ratios and DuPont Analysis

- Operating margin: 9.3% (below industry average)
  - High operating costs consuming most of revenue
- Profit margin: 4.9% (below industry average)
  - Only $4.90 kept per $100 in sales; net income over sales
- Return on Common Equity (ROE): 15.6% vs. 18.7% industry average
  - Most investor-relevant ratio; scales to any investment size
- DuPont decomposition of ROE = profit margin × total assets turnover × equity multiplier
  - Profit margin (4.9%): below average, driven by high costs, excess borrowing (interest), and limited pricing power
  - Total assets turnover (1.5x): below average, assets not generating enough sales
  - Equity multiplier (2.13x): above average, ROE partially inflated by heavy debt financing
  - ROE driven by a high multiplier (debt) rather than efficiency or profitability: a warning sign
- Market value ratios
  - P/E ratio: 11.8x vs. 13.6x industry average; investors less optimistic about future growth
  - Book value per share: $12.53 (not typically benchmarked against industry)
  - Market-to-book ratio: 1.8x (below average); market willing to pay $1.80 per $1 of book value
- EPS: use basic EPS from income statement by default; calculate (net income ÷ shares outstanding) only if not provided

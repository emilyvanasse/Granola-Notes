- Financial Accounting not a prerequisite for Managerial Accounting

  - Students who struggled in Financial can excel in Managerial, and vice versa
- Key topics covered: make-or-buy decisions, break-even analysis
- Chapters 4 and 5 skipped entirely; focus is on 1, 2, 3, and 6 for Exam 1

- Class exercises, MyLab, and attendance: 10%
- Chapter quizzes: 5%
- Each of 3 exams: 25% each

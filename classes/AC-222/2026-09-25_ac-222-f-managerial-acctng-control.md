# Chapter-by-Chapter Content Guide

- **Chapter 1** (reading chapter): managerial accounting concepts
  - Three business entities: service, manufacturing, merchandising
  - Structured data, account roles, certifications, organizations
  - Sarbanes-Oxley Act (one question)
- **Chapter 2**: product costs and inventory
  - Product costs: direct materials, direct labor, overhead
  - Period costs: selling and administrative expenses
  - Variable, fixed, and mixed costs (introduced here, deeper in Ch. 6)
  - Value chain: know all 6 components
  - Inventory accounts: raw materials, work in process, finished goods (manufacturing); finished goods only (merchandising)
  - Likely problem: cost of goods manufactured or cost of goods sold statement (pp. 82–83, 92–93)
- **Chapter 3**: job order costing
  - Job costing vs. process costing: know which business types use each
  - Job cost sheet: key focus area
  - Likely problem: similar to Exercises 23, 24, 25 (on Pearson)
  - Records used: time cards, materials requisitions
- **Chapter 6**: cost behavior and income statements
  - Fixed, variable, and mixed cost definitions and behavior
  - Fixed costs: total stays constant, per-unit changes
  - Relevant range definition
  - High-low calculation (formula on board)
  - Likely problem: one of two options:
    1. Prepare a traditional (absorption) vs. variable (contribution) costing income statement
    2. Analyze the effect of inventory changes on absorption vs. variable costing profit

# Sample MC Highlights

- CM ratio: (Selling price - Variable cost) / Selling price
- Break-even: fixed costs decrease → break-even point decreases, CM unaffected
- Margin of safety: Expected sales minus break-even sales
- Operating leverage of 1: only possible with zero fixed costs (very rare)
- Special order (Comfort Ride): $375 - $240 variable mfg cost = $135 CM × 4,100 seats = $553,500 increase
- Drop a line (Sole Sneakers): $26,000 rental income - $20,000 lost CM = $6,000 net gain
- Make or buy (Green Park): ($138,000 - $49,000 unavoidable fixed costs) / 44,000 parts = $2.02 max price
- Cash collections: include cash collected regardless of when the sale was made
- Cash sales compounding (Fletcher): $12,000 × 1.25 × 1.25 = $18,750
